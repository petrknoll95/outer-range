## Good design gives you something to feel, something to recognize, and very little to figure out.

Currently at Off Menu

### I. On simplicity

Simplicity takes judgment. Some things need removing; others need room to be complicated. The aim is to make the whole feel clear without sanding away everything that gives it character.

### II. On aesthetics

How something looks shapes how it’s understood. A little tension can make it interesting. An unexpected proportion, a sharp contrast, something slightly off balance. The work is in knowing where to push and where to let it settle.

### III. On the small things

A detail can be almost invisible and still change how the whole thing feels. Those details deserve attention. Occasionally more attention than is reasonable. The difficult part is knowing when another adjustment helps, and when it’s time to close the file.

### IV. On intention

Knowing what the work needs to do gives you room to experiment. You can question the obvious answer without losing your way. A familiar pattern should earn its place as much as an unusual one.

### V. On making

Some answers only appear once there’s something to react to. Sketching, designing, and coding are ways of thinking. The process needs enough direction to move forward and enough openness to discover a better idea along the way.
