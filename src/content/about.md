I find meaning and joy in simple things. Time with the folks. Tinkering with ideas. A long walk. A good read. Growing up in the Pyrenees taught me to appreciate the beauty of the panoramic view, the magic of the tiniest detail, and the interconnectedness of it all.

In this cosmic dance, simplicity is my guide. In both life and work, I stand for less, but better. It’s all about cutting through the noise to focus on what truly matters, in a quest for the essential.

## Principles

My philosophy is straightforward. Wherever you are and whatever you do, seek awareness and understanding, foster aesthetics, and leave the place better than you found it.
